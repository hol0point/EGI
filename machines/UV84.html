    *Do These Steps at the Start of Every Day*

  1. Turn on heaters and wait for them to reach temp.
  2. Click on the Maintenance button in the Print Control Software.
  3. Push the purge buttons as shown here
  4. Use a lint free microfiber cloth to wipe the print heads back to front.

    *Do These Steps Before Every Print*

  1. Apply thin layer of adhesion promoter to material.
  2. Move the height detector over the material. Triangulate these arrows to see where it will detect.
  3. Click the Height Control button in the Print Control Software and click Auto Detect then hit No when it asks if you want to go to specific location. Wait for it to detect height.
