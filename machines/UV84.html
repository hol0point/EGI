    *Do These Steps at the Start of Every Day*

  1. Turn on heaters and wait for them to reach temp.
  2. Click on the Maintenance button in the Print Control Software to move the carriage up.
  3. Push the purge buttons as shown here
  4. Use a lint free microfiber cloth to wipe the print heads back to front.

    *Do These Steps Before Every Print*

  • Put material face up in 0,0 position and press the vacuum.
  • Apply a thin layer of adhesion promoter to material.
  • Move the height detector over the material. Triangulate these points to see where it will detect. Where those two points intersect is where the bumper will drop to detect material thickness.
  • Click the Lifter Ctrl button in the Print Control Software and click Auto Detect then hit No when it asks if you want to go to specific location. Wait for it to detect height.
