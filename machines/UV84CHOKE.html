*Changing choke settings is necessary to fix certain print issues*

  • Raised Print - Adjust choke settings to minimize white peaking.
  • Flat Print - Adjust choke settings to minimize peaking and to help in flat print quality issues.

*Directions for changing choke settings*

  1. Open Colorbyte RIP software
  2. Double click on the Standard tab to open the settings.
